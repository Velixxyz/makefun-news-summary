World news, Wednesday 11 November 2026.

Russia and Ukraine ended a second day of US-mediated talks in Abu Dhabi with agreement in principle on a large-scale prisoner exchange, up to 1,500 prisoners each side under Red Cross facilitation, but no ceasefire, Ukrainian media reported. President Volodymyr Zelenskyy called the first day "constructive"; US Secretary of State Marco Rubio called the talks "very productive", while Russian Foreign Minister Sergei Lavrov rejected European demands for a ceasefire framework.

Attacks on shipping in the Strait of Hormuz continued: Iran's Revolutionary Guard said two tankers caught fire, and ADNOC confirmed one of its vessels was damaged in UAE waters, Reuters reported. Thirty-seven ships were attacked in October, the highest monthly total of the war, according to the Washington Institute for Near East Policy.

The US Justice Department charged three men over an alleged Iran-backed plot to attack RAF Fairford airbase in Britain. Rubio said there was no need to attack Iran again as diplomatic efforts gathered steam.

In Washington, the House voted 211-204 to block an impeachment attempt against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth. The Associated Press projected Democrats had won 254 House seats and at least 54 Senate seats, with 16 races still uncalled.

The UN COP31 climate summit opened in Antalya, Turkey, as a UN report warned of gaps in disaster warning systems. In South Korea, 57 people were hurt when a commuter bus hit 32 vehicles in Seongnam. Taiwan's TSMC reported insatiable demand for chips at the Nikkei Forum. Salvadoran troops attacked the headquarters of the 400 Mawozo gang in Haiti, Al Jazeera reported. Russia said it shot down 92 drones over the Leningrad region, and a strike cut power in Kryvyi Rih, Ukraine.
